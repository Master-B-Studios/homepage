How it works

Play the cube

Turn individual layers, inspect the puzzle from every side, and retrace your moves.

Turn a layer

Swipe across a coloured tile. The starting tile and drag direction select the row or column that rotates. Click a tile to turn its outer face.

Change the view

Press the orange rotation control, then drag the cube. Press it again to return to layer mode.

Undo or solve

The blue controls undo and redo moves. The mini-cube button rewinds the recorded history back to the solved state.

Keyboard: R, L, U, D, F and B turn outer faces; hold Shift to reverse. Arrow keys change the view. Ctrl/Cmd + Z and Y control history.
